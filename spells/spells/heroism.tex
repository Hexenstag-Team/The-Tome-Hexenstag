\spellentry{Heroism}

\tagline{Enchantment (Compulsion) [Mind-Affecting]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 2, Sor/Wiz 3
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Touch
\item[Duration:] 10 min./level
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (harmless)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (harmless)
\item[Components:] V, S
\end{description*}

This spell imbues a single creature with great bravery and morale in battle. The
target gains a +2 morale bonus on attack rolls, saves, and skill checks.
