%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Inflict Critical Wounds}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Necromancy}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 4, Destruction 4
\end{description*}

This spell functions like \textit{inflict light wounds}, except that you deal 4d8
points of damage +1 point per caster level (maximum +20).
