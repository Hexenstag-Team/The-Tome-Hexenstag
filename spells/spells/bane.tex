%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Bane}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Enchantment (Compulsion) [Fear, Mind-Affecting]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 1
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] 50 ft.
\item[Duration:] 1 min./level
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes
\item[Components:] V, S, DF
\end{description*}

Bane fills your enemies with fear and doubt. Each affected creature takes
a -1 penalty on attack rolls and a -1 penalty on saving throws against fear effects.
Bane counters and dispels \linkspell{Bless}.
