\spellentry{Invisibility, Greater}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Illusion (Glamer)}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 4, Sor/Wiz 4
\item[Duration:] 1 round/level (D)
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (harmless)
\item[Components:] V, S
\end{description*}

This spell functions like \textit{invisibility}, except that it doesn't end if
the subject attacks.
