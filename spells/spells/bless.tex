%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Bless}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Enchantment (Compulsion) [Mind-Affecting]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 1, Pal 1
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] 50 ft.
\item[Duration:] 1 min./level
\item[Saving Throw:] None
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (harmless)
\item[Components:] V, S, DF
\end{description*}

caster
Bless fills your allies with courage. Each ally gains a +1 morale bonus
on attack rolls and on saving throws against fear effects.
Bless counters and dispels Bane.
