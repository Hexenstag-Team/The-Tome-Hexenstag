\tagline{Enchantment (Compulsion) [Mind-Affecting]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 0, Sor/Wiz 0
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
\item[Duration:] 1 round
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes
\item[Components:] V, S, M
\item[Arcane Material Component:] A pinch of wool or similar substance.
\end{description*}

This enchantment clouds the mind of a humanoid creature with 4 or fewer Hit Dice
so that it takes no actions. Humanoids of 5 or more HD are not affected. A dazed
subject is not stunned, so attackers get no special advantage against it.
