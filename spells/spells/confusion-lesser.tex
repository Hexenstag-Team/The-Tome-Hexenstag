%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Confusion, Lesser}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Enchantment (Compulsion) [Mind-Affecting]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 1
\item[Range:] Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
\item[Duration:] 1 round
\item[Components:] V, S, DF
\end{description*}

This spell causes a single creature to become \linkcondition{Confused} for 1 round. See
the condition's description to determine the exact effect on the subject.
