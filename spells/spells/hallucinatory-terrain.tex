%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Hallucinatory Terrain}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Illusion (Glamer)}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 4, Sor/Wiz 4
\item[Casting Time:] 10 minutes
\item[Range:] Long (400 ft. + 40 ft./level)
\item[Duration:] 2 hours/level (D)
\item[Saving Throw:] Will disbelief (if interacted with)
\item[Spell Resistance:] No
\item[Components:] V, S, M
\item[Arcane Material Component:] A stone, a twig, and a bit of green plant.
\end{description*}

You make natural terrain look, sound, and smell like some other sort of natural
terrain. Structures, equipment, and creatures within the area are not hidden or
changed in appearance.
