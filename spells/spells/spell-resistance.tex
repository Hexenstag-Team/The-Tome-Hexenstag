%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Spell Resistance}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Abjuration}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 5, Magic 5, Protection 5
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Touch
\item[Duration:] 1 min./level
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (harmless)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (harmless)
\item[Components:] V, S, DF
\end{description*}

The creature gains spell resistance equal to 12 + your caster level.
