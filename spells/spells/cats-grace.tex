%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Cat's Grace}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Transmutation}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 2, Drd 2, Rgr 2, Sor/Wiz 2
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Touch
\item[Duration:] 1 min./level
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (harmless)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes
\item[Components:] V, S, M
\item[Arcane Material Component:] A pinch of cat fur.
\end{description*}

The transmuted creature becomes more graceful, agile, and coordinated. The spell
grants a +4 enhancement bonus to Dexterity, adding the usual benefits to AC, Reflex
saves, and other uses of the Dexterity modifier.
