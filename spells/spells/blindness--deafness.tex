%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Blindness/Deafness}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Necromancy}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 2, Clr 3, Sor/Wiz 2
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
\item[Duration:] Permanent (D)
\item[Saving Throw:] Fortitude negates
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes
\item[Components:] V
\end{description*}

You call upon the powers of unlife to render the subject blinded or deafened, as
you choose.
