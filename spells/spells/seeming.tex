%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Seeming}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Illusion (Glamer)}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 5, Sor/Wiz 5
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
\item[Duration:] 12 hours (D)
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates or Will disbelief (if interacted with)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes or No; see text
\item[Components:] V, S
\end{description*}

30 ft. apart
This spell functions like \textit{disguise self}, except that you can change the
appearance of other people as well. Affected creatures resume their normal appearances
if slain.
Unwilling targets can negate the spell's effect on them by making Will saves or
with spell resistance.
