%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Curse Water}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Necromancy [Evil]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 1
\item[Casting Time:] 1 minute
\item[Range:] Touch
\item[Duration:] Instantaneous
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (object)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (object)
\item[Components:] V, S, M
\item[Arcane Material Component:] 5 pounds of powdered silver (worth 25 gp).
\end{description*}

This spell imbues a flask (1 pint) of water with negative energy, turning it into
unholy water. Unholy water damages good outsiders the way holy water damages undead
and evil outsiders.
