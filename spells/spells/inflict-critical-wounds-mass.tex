\spellentry{Inflict Critical Wounds, Mass}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Necromancy}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 8
\end{description*}

This spell functions like \textit{mass inflict light wounds}, except that it deals
4d8 points of damage +1 point per caster level (maximum +40).
