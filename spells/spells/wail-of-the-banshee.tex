%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Wail of the Banshee}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Necromancy [Death, Sonic]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Death 9, Sor/Wiz 9
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
\item[Duration:] Instantaneous
\item[Saving Throw:] Fortitude negates
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes
\item[Components:] V
\end{description*}

You emit a terrible scream that kills creatures that hear it (except for yourself
). Creatures closest to the point of origin are affected first.
