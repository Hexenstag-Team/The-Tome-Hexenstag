%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Undetectable Alignment}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Abjuration}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Brd 1, Clr 2, Pal 2
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
\item[Duration:] 24 hours
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (object)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (object)
\item[Components:] V, S
\end{description*}

An \textit{undetectable alignment} spell conceals the alignment of an object or
a creature from all forms of divination.
