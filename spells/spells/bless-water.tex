%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Bless Water}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Transmutation [Good]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 1, Pal 1
\item[Casting Time:] 1 minute
\item[Range:] Touch
\item[Duration:] Instantaneous
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (object)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (object)
\item[Components:] V, S, M
\item[Arcane Material Component:] 5 pounds of powdered silver (worth 25 gp).
\end{description*}

This transmutation imbues a flask (1 pint) of water with positive energy, turning
it into holy water.
