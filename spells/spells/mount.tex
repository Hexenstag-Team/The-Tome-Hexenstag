%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Mount}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Conjuration (Summoning)}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Sor/Wiz 1
\item[Casting Time:] 1 round
\item[Range:] Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
\item[Effect:] One mount
\item[Duration:] 2 hours/level (D)
\item[Saving Throw:] None
\item[Spell Resistance:] No
\item[Components:] V, S, M
\item[Arcane Material Component:] A bit of horse hair.
\end{description*}

You summon a light horse or a pony (your choice) to serve you as a mount. The steed
serves willingly and well. The mount comes with a bit and bridle and a riding saddle.
