\spellentry{Doom}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Necromancy [Fear, Mind-Affecting]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 1
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./level)
\item[Duration:] 1 min./level
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes
\item[Components:] V, S, DF
\end{description*}

This spell fills a single subject with a feeling of horrible dread that causes
it to become shaken.
