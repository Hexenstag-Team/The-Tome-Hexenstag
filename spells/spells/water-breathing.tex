%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Water Breathing}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Transmutation}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 3, Drd 3, Sor/Wiz 3, Water 3
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Touch
\item[Duration:] 2 hours/level; see text
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (harmless)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (harmless)
\item[Components:] V, S, M/DF
\end{description*}

The transmuted creatures can breathe water freely. Divide the duration evenly among
all the creatures you touch.
The spell does not make creatures unable to breathe air.
Arcane Material Component: A short reed or piece of straw.
