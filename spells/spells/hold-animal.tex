\tagline{Enchantment (Compulsion) [Mind-Affecting]}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Animal 2, Drd 2, Rgr 2
\item[Components:] V, S
\end{description*}

This spell functions like \textit{hold person}, except that it affects an animal
instead of a humanoid.
