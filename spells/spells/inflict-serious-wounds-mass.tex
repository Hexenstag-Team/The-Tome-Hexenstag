%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Inflict Serious Wounds, Mass}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Necromancy}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 7
\end{description*}

This spell functions like \textit{mass inflict light wounds}, except that it deals
3d8 points of damage +1 point per caster level (maximum +35).
