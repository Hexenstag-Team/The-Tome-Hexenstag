\spellentry{Shield of Faith}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Abjuration}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 1
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Touch
\item[Duration:] 1 min./level
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (harmless)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (harmless)
\item[Components:] V, S, M
\item[Arcane Material Component:] A small parchment with a bit of holy text written
\end{description*}

This spell creates a shimmering, magical field around the touched creature that
averts attacks. The spell grants the subject a +2 deflection bonus to AC, with
an additional +1 to the bonus for every six levels you have (maximum +5 deflection
bonus at 18th level).
upon it.
