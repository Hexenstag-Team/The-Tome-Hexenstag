%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Bull's Strength}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Transmutation}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Clr 2, Drd 2, Pal 2, Sor/Wiz 2, Strength 2
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Touch
\item[Duration:] 1 min./level
\item[Saving Throw:] Will negates (harmless)
\item[Spell Resistance:] Yes (harmless)
\item[Components:] V, S, M/DF
\end{description*}

The subject becomes stronger. The spell grants a +4 enhancement bonus to Strength,
adding the usual benefits to melee attack rolls, melee damage rolls, and other
uses of the Strength modifier.
Arcane Material Component: A few hairs, or a pinch of dung, from a bull.
