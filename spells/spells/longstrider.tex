%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\spellentry{Longstrider}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\tagline{Transmutation}
\begin{description*}
\item[Level:] Drd 1, Rgr 1, Travel 1
\item[Casting Time:] 1 standard action
\item[Range:] Personal
\item[Duration:] 1 hour/level (D)
\item[Components:] V, S, M
\item[Arcane Material Component:] A pinch of dirt.
\end{description*}

This spell increases your base land speed by 10 feet. (This adjustment counts as
an enhancement bonus.) It has no effect on other modes of movement, such as burrow,
climb, fly, or swim.
